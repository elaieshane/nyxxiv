ABOUT
NYXXIV

NYXXIV is an immersive electronic music project created, produced, and founded by Tejaswanth Surisetty (performing under the alias Elaie Shane). Each track is a journey through liquid soundscapes and crimson-tinted emotions.

Moonlight is out now, and Night’s Tale is expected before November 21, 2026. Distributed by Jumpstr, the project blends atmospheric production with raw, authentic energy.

More details coming soon. Stay tuned for the full story.
